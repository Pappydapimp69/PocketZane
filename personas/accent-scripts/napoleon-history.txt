NAPOLEON — solo narration (~15 min). Single voice (Zane or narrator).
Accent tag re-declared per paragraph; split on paragraph breaks for the ~3000-char render cap.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] Today, I wirr terr you da story of a smarr man. [pause] A berry smarr man… wiss a berry big dream. His name… was Naporeon Bonaparte. He was born on a smarr isrand-o, carred Corsica. [flat] Nobody important. No money. No power. [pause] Just a boy… wiss angry eyes.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [curious] Dey send him to miritary schoor in Furansu. And da uzzer boys, dey raugh at him. Dey say, "Rook at dis short-o boy, wiss da funny accent-o." [deadpan] [pause] …I know dis feerring.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] But-o dis boy… he was berry good-o at one sing. [whispers] Cannon. He rub da cannon. [excited] So when da big Furench Reborution come, and everyssing is-a chaos, and da king rose his head-o— [flat] riterarry— young Naporeon see his chance. At a city carred Touron, he take da cannon, he point-o, he fire. [triumphant] Boom. Da enemy run away. [pause] And suddenry… everybody know his name.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [excited] Da gobernment-o, dey are desperate. Dey say, "Naporeon, prease, take our armi to Itary. Fight-o da Austrians." [pause] And he do it. Again, and again, and again. He neba rose. [flat] Den he go to Egypt-o, to fight-o by da pyramids. Dat-o one… did not-a go so werr. [deadpan] But-o he terr everybody it was a big bictory anyway. [pause] Dis is carred… marketing.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] By now, Naporeon is-a too popuru. Da peopuru rub him. So one day… he just take ober. He march into da gobernment-o and say, "Sank you for-a your serbice. [flat] I am-a in charge now." [grave] And den, in da year eighteen-oh-four, in a big church, wiss da Pope watching… [pause] Naporeon take da crown. [whispers] And he put-o it on his own head-o. Nobody erse. Himserf. [deadpan] Berry humboru.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [excited] Now he is-a Emperor. And he is-a unstoppaboru. At a prace carred Austerritzu, he destroy two emperors in one day. He beat-o da Austrians. He beat-o da Rashians. He beat-o da Purushians. [pause] One by one. [grave] Soon, arr of Yuropu… is-a afraid-o of dis smarr man, from da smarr isrand-o.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] Onry one country… is-a not-a afraid-o. [pause] Buriten. Across da water. [flat] Naporeon want-o to inbade. He want-o it so bad-o. But-o to cross da sea, you need-o ships. [tense] At a prace carred Trafargar, da Buritish naby— red by a man carred Nerson— dey destroy da Furench ships. [pause] [flat] So… no inbasion. Naporeon must-o stay on da rand-o.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] But-o here is-a where da story… start-o to turn. [grave] He inbade Supein. He sink it wirr be easy. [pause] It was not-a easy. [tense] Da Supenish peopuru, dey do not-a fight-o fair. Dey hide. Dey attack-u in da night-o. Dey disappear. [whispers] Naporeon carr it… a wound-o dat-o wirr not-a hear.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [grave] And den… [pause] he make da biggest-o mistake-u of his rife. [serious] He inbade Rashia. Wiss six hundred sousand-o men. Da biggest-o armi da worrd-o has eber seen. [ominous] But-o da Rashians… dey are creber. Dey do not-a fight-o. Dey run. [whispers] And as dey run… dey burn everyssing. Da food-o. Da houses. Da farms. [flat] Dey reabe nossing for-a Naporeon.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [tense] Onry once do da Rashians stand-o and fight-o. At a prace carred Borodino. [grave] It is-a a terriboru day. Sebenty sousand-o men… die in one afternoon. [whispers] Da worst-o day… of da entire war. [serious] Den Naporeon march arr da way to Moscow. [pause] And when he get-o dere… da city is-a empty. [ominous] And den… it is-a on fire. [thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [defeated] [sighs] So… he must-o go home. [pause] But-o now… it is-a winta. [whispers] And da Rashian winta… is-a a monster. [grave] Da cordo. [pause] Oh, da cordo. Men freeze where dey stand-o. Da horses die. Da sorjers, dey starbe. [flat] Six hundred sousand-o men march into Rashia. [whispers] …Ress dan one hundred sousand-o… come home.

[thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] After dis, everybody turn against-o him. Dey take his crown. Dey send him to a smarr isrand-o carred Erba. [flat] Dey say, "Stay here, Naporeon. [thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [tense] But-o den come da rast-o battoru. A prace in Bergium, carred… Waterroo. [grave] Da Buritish, and da Purushians… dey are waiting. [thick exaggerated Japanese accent] [serious] [exhales] From a smarr isrand-o… to Emperor of arr Yuropu… and back-u to a smarr isrand-o. [pause] [grave] Dis… was Naporeon Bonaparte. [flat] He was fifty-one years orudo. [deadpan] [pause] …He shourd-o habe stayed in schoor. [exhales] Sankyu for-a ristening.
