# LOCKED GOAL (loop 6)

Fixed end-state specification for AGAIN. The only bar the independent checker
measures against. Not edited during the loop. Amended between loops by the
ratchet only.

## The intended end state (observable terms)

A noir interrogation system where the suspect **reacts, lies, and lies back**.
Lies are **introduced through questioning** and **grow under pressure**. Every
case **differs meaningfully**. All content is **generated in code** and
**verified as solvable**. The system includes an **optimization layer** and
preserves **PW's gotcha, escalation, and theater** without sacrificing
**generativity**, the **keystone**, or the principle that **"a lie can't tell
itself the same way twice."**

## Ratchet amendment (loop 2 — met, retained)

Act 1 must generate at least one contradiction, instability, or vulnerability
from the suspect's OWN answers before external evidence is introduced. That
instability must be stored by the system and usable later as interrogation
leverage.

## Ratchet amendment (loop 3 — met, retained)

The contradiction must be the player's to DISCOVER. At standard difficulty the
game must NOT announce it — it presents his answers plainly and the player must
notice the conflict and act. The system still stores it and keeps it usable, and
the case stays winnable for a player who never notices. Only the lowest (lenient)
difficulty names the conflict and what to do.

## Ratchet amendment (loop 4 — met, retained)

The keystone must reach Act 1: in a keystone case the interview lets the player
surface a suspicion about the corroborating witness from his own answers, carried
into the confrontation as a steer — without pre-breaking it or spoiling the
cascade. Non-keystone cases show no such suspicion.

## Ratchet amendment (loop 5 — met, retained)

The interview must not play the same shape every case. Beyond the one guaranteed
self-contradiction, the composition of the remaining questions must VARY by seed
— some cases give a second own-words contradiction, others a record (lever) to
work with — so the right line of questioning differs case to case. Every case
still carries at least one self-contradiction, and every case stays solvable.

## Ratchet amendment (loop 6)

**The two questions the detective leaves unasked must surface in the
confrontation as the suspect's cover. Five questions, three rounds — two doors
always go unopened. Entering Act 2, the game must reveal which two went unasked
and, for any that were productive (a lie, lever, tell, or the keystone), present
that closed door as a steer toward the prop it concerned: the ground he was
relieved you never walked. It is a revealed dramatic beat, not silent
bookkeeping. It must NOT hand a free break — the prop still has to be worked
through the web, consistent with "you cannot press without evidence" — and the
case stays winnable whichever two questions go unasked.**
